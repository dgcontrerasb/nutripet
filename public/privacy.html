Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Applies to NutriPet OTB (nutripet-v2.vercel.app) and NutriPet TikTok Publisher

1. Information We Collect

  • NutriPet Web Application: Account authentication details (such as email and display name via Firebase Authentication) and pet profile data voluntarily entered by the user (pet name, breed, weight, reminders, and optional emergency contact info).
  • NutriPet TikTok Publisher Integration: When an authorized NutriPet administrator connects our official TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit, we receive only the minimum OAuth tokens and basic profile information (user.info.basic) required to identify the connected account and publish educational videos using the Content Posting API (video.publish).

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide pet nutrition estimation, health tracking, and emergency QR card features in NutriPet.
  • To authenticate our authorized TikTok account and publish finalized educational pet care videos directly from NutriPet's internal publishing panel.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share user data or TikTok account data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Application data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication) with per-user access isolation. TikTok OAuth credentials and tokens are stored exclusively in protected server/local environment storage and are never exposed in client-side code or public repositories.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You may delete your NutriPet account and associated pet records at any time from within the application or by contacting us. TikTok OAuth access can be revoked at any time from your TikTok account settings (Manage app permissions), after which stored tokens are invalidated and deleted.

5. Third-Party Services

NutriPet integrates with Google Firebase (authentication and database), Vercel (hosting), and TikTok APIs (for internal video publishing). Each service operates under its own privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

NutriPet is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Contact & Privacy Requests

For privacy questions, data access, or deletion requests, contact us at nutripet.v2@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at @dgcontrerasb.
